From measured motion to a mathematical model.

Collect a few observations, plot them, and investigate what a best-fit equation says about a moving object. Begin with sample data or enter measurements of your own.

Your measurements stay on this device. This demonstration analyzes numbers locally in your browser—no account, file upload, or external AI service.
01 · Think first

What pattern do you expect?

If a cart's position increases by the same distance during each one-second interval, what shape should its position–time graph have? What if those distances increase each second?

A straight line has constant slope, which represents constant velocity.

A curved graph may indicate changing velocity. For constant acceleration, a quadratic position model is appropriate.

Residuals: where the model misses

A residual is measured position minus predicted position. Ideally, residuals are small and show no systematic pattern.
