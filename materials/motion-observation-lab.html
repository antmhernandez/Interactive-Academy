Motion Observation Lab

Name: Date:
Purpose

Observe, measure and describe movement. Relate equal time intervals to distance traveled and changes in average speed.

Materials

Measuring tape or meterstick, a small rolling object, a safe flat surface, timer or video-recording device, paper and pencil.

1. Predict

How will the distance traveled during each second change if a cart gradually speeds up?

2. Observe

On a safe, level surface, roll an object several times. Mark or record its position at equal time intervals. Repeat as needed to obtain usable observations. Record actual measurements; do not invent missing observations.

3. Interpret

  1. Draw a motion diagram from your measurements.
  2. Did your object appear to travel at constant speed? What evidence supports your answer?
  3. Calculate the average speed over the entire observed interval. Show quantities and units.

4. Consider error

What is one likely measurement limitation in your method, and how could you improve it?

Safety: Keep rolling objects away from stairs, roads and traffic. Do not chase objects into hazardous areas. Original teaching demonstration; not an official school laboratory assessment.
