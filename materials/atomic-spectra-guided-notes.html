How Can Light Identify an Atom?

Name Date

1. Observe before explaining

White light spread through a prism produces many colors. Light from a low-pressure excited gas can produce separate bright lines instead. Why might particular colors be missing?

Complete the idea.

An atom emits a photon when it changes from a state of energy to one of energy.

Each emitted photon carries the energy between the two states.
